# Page topology

Visual order at 1440px, page height ~10521px. Cream page `#E9EBDF`, forest text `#284010`, lime accent `#78BF30`.

1. **SiteHeader** (absolute overlay, ~168px) — lime info bar + wordmark + Menu/Place/Visit. Overlays the hero.
2. **HeroSection** (900px, padding-top 240px) — headline, two CTAs, cold-matcha photo, product card, doodle illustrations.
3. **TickerMarquee** — repeating “Good coffee / No coffee snobbery / …” with star illustrations. Appears between several sections.
4. **FeaturedMenuSection** (“Coffee this way / Choose your cup of happy”) — 2x2-ish mosaic of four featured items.
5. **FullMenuSection** (#menu) — six category columns with size/price rows.
6. **MoodMatcherSection** (black/forest) — click-driven mood wheel + drink result cards.
7. **CoffeeOclockSection** — click/tab timeline (07:03, 10:12, 14:47, 17:31) with copy.
8. **WhatMattersSection** (forest) — receipt card + CTAs.
9. **PlaceSection** (#place) — click-driven seating spots carousel (01–04).
10. **ReviewsSection** (#reviews) — testimonial slider.
11. **VisitSection** (#visit, forest) — photo collage + location/hours.
12. **SiteFooter** — logo, legal, Explore/Follow/Contact columns.

## Interaction models
- Tickers: time-driven CSS marquee
- Mood matcher: click-driven
- Coffee o’clock: click-driven tabs (also shows a clock)
- Place spots: click-driven
- Reviews: click-driven slider
- Header: static overlay (no Lenis / locomotive)
